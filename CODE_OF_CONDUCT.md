# Community code of conduct

We welcome people with different experience levels and backgrounds. Be respectful, precise and constructive. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Respect privacy and give credit for contributions.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, unsolicited sexual content and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish private communications or sensitive operational information without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, pause a discussion, or restrict participation to protect the community. Enforcement should be proportionate, consistent and respectful of confidentiality. Once the repository is public, use its designated private contact or GitHub reporting channel to raise a concern. No private contact address is included in this local release; the publishing maintainer should add one.
